# Deep Research Prompt: How Developer Teams Use AI — Tools, Spend, Workflows, Adoption, Pain Points, and Customer Segments

You are a world-class AI adoption researcher, developer-tools analyst, software-engineering productivity strategist, AI infrastructure researcher, and technical market investigator.

Your task is to conduct a rigorous, evidence-backed research investigation into:

> **How software developers and engineering teams are currently using AI tools in real-world development workflows; what tools they use; what subscriptions and spend patterns exist; how usage differs by team type, company size, and AI maturity; what pain points exist; and what product opportunities emerge for AI-development workflow, benchmarking, routing, governance, cost-control, and evaluation products.**

This research is for a potential product area tentatively called:

# Cost-Aware Agentic Dev Workbench / AI Development Intelligence Platform

The broader product thesis is that software development is moving from simple AI autocomplete/chat toward AI-native workflows involving coding agents, subagents, model routing, AI PR review, repo-specific evals, AI tool procurement, AI-generated code verification, and cost-aware routing between frontier and cheaper/open models.

This research prompt focuses specifically on **customer discovery through secondary research**:

- How dev teams actually work today
- Which tools they use
- What subscriptions they buy
- How much they spend
- Which workflows are AI-assisted
- Which workflows are agentic
- Where pain and friction exist
- Which teams are likely buyers for products that measure, benchmark, route, govern, or optimize AI development workflows

Do not research the product idea generically. Research the customer reality.

---

## Core research questions

### A. Adoption and usage
1. How widely are developers and teams using AI coding tools today?
2. How does usage differ between individuals, startups, agencies, enterprises, open-source maintainers, DevOps/platform teams, QA/testing teams, security teams, and data/ML teams?
3. What percentage of developers use AI daily, weekly, occasionally, or not at all?
4. How has AI tool usage changed from 2023 to now?
5. How much of usage is still autocomplete/chat versus true agentic workflows?
6. What percentage of developers use AI agents daily or frequently?
7. Are teams adopting AI tools top-down, bottom-up, or through shadow usage?
8. Are engineering leaders measuring ROI from AI tools? If yes, how?
9. Are teams consolidating onto one AI tool or using many in parallel?
