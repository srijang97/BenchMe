# Internet-Only Validation Research Specification

This is the internet-only research programme we designed to validate the idea before primary interviews or deep product buildout.

## Core decision

Is there a real business opportunity around:
- repo-specific AI benchmarking
- AI dev-tool procurement intelligence
- workflow observability
- verification evidence
- later cost-aware routing

We are not trying to prove “AI coding is growing.” We are trying to prove:
1. teams use multiple AI dev tools
2. teams are confused about which tools/models are best
3. teams have cost / quality / verification / governance pain
4. existing tools do not solve neutral repo-specific evaluation
5. a differentiated product wedge exists
