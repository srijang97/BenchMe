Model notes. Weaker models are assumed to burn more tokens (frontier 1.0×, open 1.45×, cheap 2.10×) and solve fewer tasks (60% / 50% / 33%) — both measured directionally in the demo run, where gpt-5.4-mini used 2.12× the input tokens of gpt-5.4 on the same task. Cost per solved task therefore includes the wasted spend on failures. Non-API compute (containers) is ~2% of total and excluded. Cross-checked against Databricks ($1.28–1.94/task) and HAL ($0.13–0.73/task); this model runs 1.5–2× conservative, so treat outputs as an upper bound.
